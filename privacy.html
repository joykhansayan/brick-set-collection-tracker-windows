Privacy Policy

Brick Set Collection Tracker for Windows · Last updated: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brick Set Collection Tracker for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

The sets and minifigures you record (names, numbers, themes, prices, values, storage notes, dates), any photos you attach and your preferences are stored only in the App's local storage on your Windows device. They never leave your device unless you choose to export a CSV or PDF file yourself, and they are removed when you uninstall the App or choose Erase All Data in Settings.

3. Photos

Photos are added only when you pick an image file yourself. The App stores a downscaled copy locally and does not access your camera, microphone or photo library otherwise.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Network use

The App works offline. It connects to the internet only to download app updates, to let the Microsoft Store verify your purchases, and when you choose to open an external link in your browser.

6. Children

The App is a general-audience collection tracker for adult collectors. Because it collects no personal data at all, it does not collect personal data from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
